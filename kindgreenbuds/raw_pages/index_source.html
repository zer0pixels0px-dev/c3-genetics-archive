KindGreenBuds.com is home to the web’s longest-running marijuana strain library, originally created by medical cannabis enthusiasts in 2007. Explore detailed cannabis strain information covering genetics, lineage, breeder history, effects, THC and CBD levels, terpene profiles, flavors, aromas, and growing characteristics. From classic indica and sativa varieties to modern hybrids and rare cultivars, our strain database is designed to help you research and compare marijuana strains in one comprehensive resource.

INDICA STRAINS

Indica strains are cannabis varieties commonly associated with relaxing, body-focused effects, compact growth, and dense, resinous flowers. Many trace their genetics to regions such as Afghanistan, Pakistan, and the Hindu Kush, and they remain popular among growers and consumers for their potency, rich terpene profiles, and calming character.

Explore All Indica Strains >>

SATIVA STRAINS

Sativa strains are cannabis varieties commonly associated with uplifting, energetic, and mentally stimulating effects, along with taller growth and narrower leaves. Many trace their roots to warm regions of Africa, Southeast Asia, and Central and South America, and they remain popular for their distinctive terpene profiles and cerebral character.

Explore All Sativa Strains >>

HYBRID STRAINS

Hybrid strains are cannabis varieties created by crossing different genetic lines, often combining traits associated with both indica and sativa plants. They can offer a wide range of effects, flavors, terpene profiles, and growing characteristics, making hybrids some of the most diverse and popular strains in modern cannabis.

Explore All Hybrid Strains >>

LANDRACE STRAINS

Landrace strains are traditional cannabis varieties that developed naturally in specific regions over generations, adapting to local climates and growing conditions. Valued for their genetic heritage and distinctive regional traits, landrace strains form the foundation of many modern indica, sativa, and hybrid cultivars.

Explore All Landrace Strains >>

AUTOFLOWERING STRAINS

Autoflowering strains are cannabis varieties that begin flowering automatically with age rather than changes in the light cycle. Known for compact growth, fast harvest times, and easy cultivation, autoflower strains are popular with beginners, small-space growers, and anyone looking for a quicker seed-to-harvest cycle.
